Overview
Welcome to Classification Rumble — Neurontara Data Clash 2025
Can your model tell a good freshwater measurement from a fair or unknown one?
In this skills-based Kaggle challenge you will build a classifier that predicts the quality grade assigned to individual water-quality records collected around the globe.

🏆 Objective
Predict the categorical target Data Quality (Good, Fair, Unknown, Pending Review) for each row in the test set. Submissions are ranked by macro-averaged F1 on a hidden private hold-out.

🌍 Dataset at a Glance
Theme	Examples of columns	Why it matters
In-situ readings	Parameter Code, value_with_unit, temperature_avg, rainfall_mm, …	The raw chemical / physical measurement.
Lab workflow	Analysis Method Code, testing_laboratory, sampling_method, validated_results	Certain methods or labs produce systematically higher (or lower) QC grades.
Location & ecology	Latitude, Longitude, water_body_type, urban_pct, agriculture_pct, frag_index	Catchment land-use and fragmentation influence contamination risk.
Regulatory context	analytical_program, regulatory_framework, Dataset_Source	Different agencies apply different quality criteria.
Source: UNEP GEMS/Water Global Freshwater Quality Archive (1906-2023) by Saile (2024) + Private External Sources

Evaluation
Submissions are evaluated on macro F1-score between your predicted categorical label and the hidden ground-truth label for every record in the test set.

Macro‐F1 gives equal weight to every class (Good, Fair, Unknown, Pending Review), so strong performance on the minority classes is essential for a high score.

Target
Column	What you predict	Classes
Data Quality	Overall quality rating assigned by the domain authority to the measurement row. It is a four-class categorical variable.	Fair, Unknown, Good, Pending review
